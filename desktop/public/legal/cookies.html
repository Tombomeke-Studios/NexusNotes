NexusNotes

Cookie Policy

Version 2026-10-03

NexusNotes sets no cookies. It keeps a few items in your browser’s local storage, all strictly necessary for the app to work. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking, so nothing is stored for those purposes.

What is stored

ItemPurposeKept until
nexus_token, nexus_refreshKeeps you signed inYou sign out, or after 30 days unused
nexus_device_idTells your devices apart for syncYou clear site data
nexus_workspace_prefsYour layout, font size, motion and templatesYou clear site data
Drafts, recent notes, empty foldersNot losing unsaved text; the Recent list; folders without notes yet (never for end-to-end encrypted vaults’ text)Saved, or you clear site data
nexus_checklist, nexus_tips_seenWhich first-run steps and tips you have seenYou clear site data
nexus_consentYour storage preferences from the bannerYou clear site data

Your choices

Necessary storage cannot be switched off while you use the app. The “Cookie preferences” link on the sign-in screen and in Settings → Account shows these choices again; if optional categories (analytics, marketing) are ever added, they stay off until you turn them on there. You can remove everything by signing out and clearing this site’s data in your browser.
