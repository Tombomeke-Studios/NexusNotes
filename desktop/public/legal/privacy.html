How your notes are protected

Everything you store is encrypted at rest on the server. Vaults are end-to-end encrypted by default: their notes, titles, folder names, attachments and linked files are encrypted on your device, and the server (including us) cannot read them. In a vault you chose not to end-to-end encrypt, the server can read your notes to provide search and other features.
